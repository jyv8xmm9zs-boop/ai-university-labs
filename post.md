**Understanding AI Agents: A Beginner's Guide**

AI agents are intelligent systems that operate either autonomously or with little human oversight. They observe their environment, make decisions, and act to achieve specific goals based on rules or algorithms.

There are three main types of AI agents: 

1. **Reactive Agents**: These respond directly to immediate situations. A good example is a spam filter, which quickly identifies and blocks unwanted emails.

2. **Strategic Agents**: Unlike reactive agents, these plan actions over time. For instance, navigation apps like Google Maps strategize the best routes based on traffic patterns.

3. **Learning Agents**: These improve their performance through experience. Netflix’s recommendation system is a prime example, as it learns your viewing habits to suggest shows you might like.

As we embrace these technologies, we must also address ethical concerns such as accountability and bias. It's crucial to think critically about the impact of AI on jobs and society as we integrate these systems into our daily lives.
